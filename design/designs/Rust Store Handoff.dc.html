Routes, rendering and islands

Everything is server-rendered by default. Sorting, filtering and pagination are plain links with query params, and every mutation is a <form method="post"> handled in the route, so pages work without JS. Islands only improve three things: the quantity stepper, filter/sort controls that submit on change, and status actions that report a 409 inline without a full reload.
